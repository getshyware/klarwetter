Klarwetter shows the weather for your places – right now, hour by hour and for the coming days. No ads, no account and no access to your location.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Current weather with feels-like temperature, humidity and wind</li>
<li>Hourly temperature, chance of rain and wind</li>
<li>Forecast for up to 9 days</li>
<li>Up to 10 places – search by name or postal code</li>
<li>Home screen widget: page through your places or stretch it to see several side by side</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
<ul>
<li>Only permission: Internet</li>
<li>No location access – you choose your places yourself; they stay on the device</li>
<li>No ads, no analytics, no third-party libraries</li>
</ul>

Weather data: Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Place search: Open-Meteo and OpenStreetMap Nominatim (© OpenStreetMap contributors). Only the searched place or its coordinates are sent.

The app is currently available in German.
